🎼 How TuneVault Works

TuneVault focuses on music uploaded by its users.

1. Open TuneVault through the live website or installed Android application.
2. Upload a supported audio file using the available upload functionality.
3. Select a song from your available music collection.
4. Start playback and enjoy your music.

Important: No Built-in Song Library

TuneVault does not provide a preloaded collection of songs or an inbuilt music catalogue.

Users must supply their own supported audio files. TuneVault is intended for listening to music that users have the right to upload and stream.
